What is Relationsight?

Clarity for when something needs to change.

Relationsight is for people whose relationship needs a change but aren't sure what kind. Whether you're working to improve things or honestly not sure if you're in the right place, Relationsight gives you research-backed tools to find clarity and move forward.

The app adapts to your situation. If you're trying to strengthen your relationship, you'll see content focused on connection and repair. If you're evaluating whether to stay, you'll see content focused on honest assessment and decision clarity.

Features

Everything you need to see clearly.

  • Daily Ledger Journal entries, daily check-ins, behavioural counters, and commitments to track your relationship over time.
  • Research-backed lessons 35+ lessons based on research by Gottman, Rusbult, and Joel, with exercises to put what you learn into practice.
  • Adaptive content The app adjusts to whether you're improving your relationship or deciding what comes next.
  • Partner insights Personality profiles and AI-powered conversation analysis from WhatsApp chat exports.
  • Community Anonymous discussion with people going through the same thing, tied to the lessons you've completed.
  • Trend visualisations See how your relationship is changing over weeks and months with check-in trends and weekly activity.
The research

Every lesson in Relationsight cites its source. The content draws on John Gottman's 40 years of couple studies, Caryl Rusbult's Investment Model of commitment, and Samantha Joel's machine learning analysis of 11,000+ couples.

Privacy

Your data is yours.

Journal entries, check-ins, and partner profiles are private to your account. Imported conversations are processed for insights and never stored. Community responses use first names only. Read the full privacy policy.
