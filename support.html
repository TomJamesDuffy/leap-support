Frequently asked questions

  • How do I delete my account?

    Go to the Profile tab, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Delete account." This permanently removes your profile, journal entries, and all associated data.

  • Is my data private?

    Yes. Your journal entries, check-ins, partner profile, and personal details are only visible to you. Community responses are shared anonymously using first names only. Imported WhatsApp conversations are processed for insights and never stored.

  • What is "improve" vs "decide" mode?

    During onboarding, Leap asks how you're feeling about your relationship. Based on your answer, the app adapts its content. Improve mode focuses on strengthening your connection. Decide mode focuses on honest evaluation and clarity.

  • Can I change my mode?

    Your mode is based on your onboarding responses. If your situation has changed, contact us and we can help you update it.

  • How does the conversation analysis work?

    You can export a WhatsApp chat and import it into Leap. The app parses the conversation on your device, then uses AI to generate insights based on your relationship context. The conversation itself is never saved to our servers.

  • Is Leap a substitute for therapy?

    No. Leap is a self-reflection and tracking tool based on relationship research. It is not therapy, counselling, or medical advice. If you or your partner are in crisis, please contact a licensed professional.
